# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be decent. This is a small, focused project about keeping people's belongings
safe — bring the same care to the community.

- **Constructive > critical.** Review code, not people. "This connector
  violates the geometry rule" beats "this is sloppy".
- **Assume competence.** Questions are welcome; so are strong opinions, held
  loosely.
- **Keep the demo honest.** We don't ship dead buttons or fake claims —
  hold contributions (and maintainers) to that standard.
- **No harassment** of any kind: sexism, racism, trolling, personal attacks,
  or unwelcome attention. Zero tolerance.

## Scope

Applies to the repository (issues, PRs, discussions) and any project space
(Discord, demos, conference talks representing Trackio).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this
policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or
repeated violations. Report problems via
[GitHub contact](https://github.com/abdou-da0wew) — reports are handled
confidentially.
